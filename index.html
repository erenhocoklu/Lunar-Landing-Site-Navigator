What is simulated here and what the competition version would use

Terrain
Generated: a 21 km crater near the pole and a massif toward Earth, on a 48 × 48 km map. Next: real LOLA heights from NASA PGDA (LDEM_80S_*_ADJ), baked by our Python engine.
Sun and Earth
Real: one JPL Horizons table (2026-2030, every 6 h) of the sub-solar and sub-Earth points in the Moon's mean-Earth frame. The page interpolates it and computes the Sun and Earth direction from any site, including Earth parallax. Checked against Horizons topocentric values.
Computation
3D demo: each point's horizon in 64 directions out to 46 km, with the Moon's curvature. Real sites: 360-direction horizon masks from LOLA terrain, computed by our Python engine (flat horizon until they load).
