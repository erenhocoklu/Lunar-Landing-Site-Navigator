# Engine: permanent-shadow layer (psr.json + psr_km)

Branch `engine`, worktree `engine-wt`, uncommitted. `pipeline/build_psr.py`.

- Source: PGDA 90 `LPSR_80S_20MPP_ADJ` shapefile (SHP+SHX+DBF+PRJ, ~287 MB, kept in `data/psr/` outside git).
  506,349 polygons at 20 m; kept the **1,531 >= 1 km^2** (matches PGDA's own large-PSR count, MEASURED), total 18,921 km^2.
- `data/psr.json`: outlines simplified at 120 m, coordinates rounded to 10 m, **1.00 MB**. Label "permanently shadowed (LOLA model)".
- `psr_km` in sites.json = straight-line distance to the edge of the nearest PSR >= 1 km^2 (full 20 m outlines), 0 if inside:
  A C1 6.06 km, B S 2.43 km, C GR1 7.17 km, D MP1 12.74 km (MEASURED).
- Checks: Shackleton crater floor -> 0.0 km; C1 > 1 km; 12 tests pass.
- Why >= 1 km^2: smaller PSRs are tiny pockets next to almost every rim; including them makes "distance to shadow" near 0 everywhere.
- Minor: 2 shapes had orphan hole rings; pyshp kept them as outlines (display only, negligible).
- Not ice. Never write "ice" or "distance to ice" on the page.
